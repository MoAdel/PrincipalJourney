# Curated Resources & Landmark AI Papers

### Landmark Academic Papers
- **ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models** (Yao et al., 2022) — *The foundational paper establishing the ReAct agent framework.*
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks** (Lewis et al., 2020) — *The original RAG paper from Meta AI.*
- **Efficient and Robust Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search Using Hierarchical Navigable Small World Graphs** (Malkov & Yashunin, 2016) — *The mathematical foundation of HNSW.*
- **Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts** (Liu et al., Stanford, 2023) — *Proving why large context windows fail without targeted retrieval.*

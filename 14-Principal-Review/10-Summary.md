# Sprint 14 Executive Summary & The Principal Engineer Creed

---

## The Principal Engineer Creed

1. **We design for failure**: Everything fails eventually. Hardware, networks, third-party APIs, and human developers will all fail simultaneously. Our architectures survive gracefully.
2. **We make trade-offs explicit**: There is no best architecture—only trade-offs between consistency, availability, latency, complexity, and operational cost. We record every trade-off in Architecture Decision Records.
3. **We eliminate single points of failure**: In our systems, our data pipelines, and our teams.
4. **We automate governance**: We never rely on human vigilance where a compiler check, unit test, or automated CI pipeline can enforce an architectural rule.
5. **We lead through influence, clarity, and empathy**: A Principal Engineer does not command; a Principal Engineer elevates the technical competence and confidence of every engineer in the organization.
